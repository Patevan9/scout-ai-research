Glucose Glance Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Glucose Glance app for Wear OS and the Glucose Glance Watch Face (together, "the app") handle your information. The app is made by Patrick Evan Lippy, Lippy Robotics Labs ("we", "us").

The short version

  • Lippy Robotics Labs has no servers and never receives your glucose readings or your login. We have no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app does send your login and requests to the glucose service you choose, so it can read your readings.
  • Your login and your glucose readings are stored only on your watch.
  • Your password or access token is encrypted on the watch.
  • The app connects only to the glucose service you choose (Dexcom Share or your Nightscout site), over secure HTTPS, to read your own glucose numbers.
  • If you buy Glucose Glance on our website, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your email address and purchase details, but never your card number. Your purchase is never linked to your glucose readings. The app checks your license on your watch and never contacts us or Stripe to do it.

The app does not ask for or use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, the watch's health sensors, or any advertising ID. It uses internet access to reach your glucose service, plus standard Android background permissions so it can refresh your glucose about every 5 minutes, including after the watch restarts.

Dexcom Share

If you choose Dexcom, the app reads your glucose from Dexcom Share, the same service Dexcom's own "Follow" feature uses. Share must be turned on in your Dexcom app.

  • The app sends your Dexcom username and password to Dexcom's Share server to sign in. Dexcom returns a session ID.
  • The app then uses the session ID to ask Dexcom for your readings from the last 3 hours, about every 5 minutes.
  • Depending on the region you pick, the app connects to share2.dexcom.com (United States), shareous1.dexcom.com (outside the United States) or share.dexcom.jp (Japan).
  • Your glucose data is held by Dexcom under Dexcom's privacy policy. We do not receive it.

Glucose Glance is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Dexcom, Inc.

Nightscout

If you choose Nightscout, the app reads your glucose from the Nightscout site whose address you enter. Nightscout is open-source software that you, or someone you trust, runs.

  • The app asks your site for its most recent readings (up to 48) and keeps the ones from the last 3 hours.
  • If you enter an access token, the app includes it in its request so your site lets it read your readings. If your site rejects it, the app tries it as your site's API secret, which it sends as a hash (SHA-1), the way Nightscout expects.
  • Because the access token is part of the web address the app requests, it may appear in your Nightscout server's logs, which whoever runs your site can see.
  • Whoever runs your Nightscout site controls that data and its privacy. We have no access to your site.

Glucose Glance is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by the Nightscout project.

Demo mode

Demo mode shows simulated glucose readings created on your watch. They are SIMULATED — NOT REAL health data and must not be used for treatment.

  • Demo mode does not use any account or internet connection, and nothing is sent.
  • Simulated readings are not stored, and they are kept separate from your real readings.
  • Turning Demo mode off returns to your saved glucose service.

Encryption and security

  • On your watch: your Dexcom password and your Nightscout access token or API secret are encrypted with AES-256 (GCM), using a key kept in your watch's secure Android Keystore. They are never stored as plain text.
  • Other saved information (your Dexcom username or Nightscout site address, the temporary Dexcom session ID, your recent readings and your settings) is kept in the app's private storage. Android keeps this storage separate from other apps.
  • Over the internet: the app connects only over encrypted HTTPS. Android blocks this app from using unencrypted connections.
  • Backups: the app turns off Android's cloud backup, so its data is not copied to Google Drive by the app.

Third parties and other connections

We never receive your glucose readings or your glucose service login, and we do not sell or rent any of your information. The app sends information only in the ways listed below, each because you chose to use that feature. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting software. These are the only outside parties involved:

  • The glucose service you choose: Dexcom (Dexcom Share) or your own Nightscout site, as described above.
  • Watch faces you choose: Glucose Glance can show your glucose on a watch face as a "complication". When you add it to a watch face, including faces made by other developers, that watch face receives your current glucose number, trend and the 3-hour chart so it can display them. Only add it to watch faces you trust.
  • Read aloud: when you tap the number to hear it, the app uses your watch's text-to-speech service to speak your glucose. That service is part of your watch's software, and its own privacy terms apply.
  • Stripe: when you buy the app on our website, Stripe processes your payment, as described in "Buying Glucose Glance" below.

Buying Glucose Glance

Glucose Glance is sold on our website, lippyroboticslabs.com/glucose-glance. Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc.

  • What you give Stripe: on Stripe's secure checkout page, you enter your email address and your payment details, such as your card number, expiry date and security code. Depending on your payment method, Stripe may also ask for your name, country or postal code.
  • What we receive from Stripe: your email address, the name and billing details you entered, what you bought, the amount, the date, and limited card details such as the card type and the last four digits. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your license: after you buy, we create a license for you. It contains a random purchase ID (not based on your email), a masked version of your email, the license type, the date and a digital signature. The installer app on your phone only carries the license to your watch. Your license never expires, and the app never checks it online. We keep a record that links your purchase ID to your Stripe payment, so we can restore your purchase if you get a new watch.
  • Why we use it: to send you the download link and install instructions, to give you a receipt, to help you if you contact us (for example, about a refund or reinstalling on a new watch), and to keep the business and tax records the law requires.
  • What we do not do: we do not sell or rent your purchase information, we do not send you marketing emails unless you ask, and we never connect your purchase to your glucose readings or login. The app on your watch does not send anything to us.
  • Email: we write to you from lippyroboticslabs@gmail.com, which uses Google's Gmail service.
  • Stripe's own policy: Stripe handles your payment details under the Stripe Privacy Policy, and also uses them to prevent fraud.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Readings are kept for about 3 hours and replaced as new ones arrive.
  • Changing your login in the app replaces your old login and clears the saved readings and session ID.
  • To delete everything the app stored, uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your watch's settings (Settings → Apps → Glucose Glance → Storage → Clear data; the exact menu names can vary by watch). This also removes your license from that watch. Email us and we can restore it.
  • We never receive your glucose readings or login, so there is nothing of that kind for us to delete. To delete data held by Dexcom or your Nightscout site, contact Dexcom or whoever runs your site.
  • If you bought the app, we keep your purchase information, including the record that links your purchase ID to your Stripe payment, for as long as we need it to support you and to keep the records the law requires (for tax records, this is usually several years). To ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to delete anything we are not required to keep, email us.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Health notice

Glucose Glance is not a medical device. It shows a copy of readings from your glucose service, and those readings can be late, missing or wrong. Always keep your CGM's own app turned on for alarms, and do not make treatment decisions based on this app alone.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will update the policy before the app changes.
