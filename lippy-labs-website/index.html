Tolliver

A quiet presence.

Tolliver runs a local AI brain right on your device. It recognizes the people in your home, remembers what matters to you, and answers in its own friendly voice — all offline by default.

  • Runs on-device — works without the internet
  • No account, no subscription, no data harvesting
  • Real memory: facts it keeps and habits it learns
  • Built and tested on real phones, every release

About the lab

Small studio. Big care.

Lippy Robotics Labs is an independent studio run by Patrick Evan Lippy. It started with a simple question: what if an old Android phone could become a friendly, private companion for the home? That question became Tolliver.

Today the lab builds apps and games that share the same values — run on the device, respect the user, and get tested on real phones before anyone else sees them.
