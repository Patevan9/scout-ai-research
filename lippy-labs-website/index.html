Rollabop

The physical pile is the puzzle.

In closed testing · Android
Become a Beta Tester

A free-to-play physics puzzle game set inside a tall walnut tray full of polished glass balls. Nothing is locked in place. Every ball has weight, and every move changes the pile. Reach in, drag a ball through the crowd, and find the move that sets off a cascade.

  • Gravity is always on.Balls fall, bump, roll and settle naturally.
  • Touch any ball.Drag it anywhere. The others move out of the way.
  • No timers. No rush.The challenge comes from the puzzle, never the clock.
Fair by design. Every level can be won without paying and without watching an ad. Any ads are optional, clearly labelled and never interrupt your game.

Tolliver

A companion, not another chatbot.

In development · Not yet available

Tolliver began as Scout, our original attempt at a private AI companion that runs on the phone itself instead of in someone else's cloud. Scout grew into Tolliver.

The goal is something that feels present in your home, with its own personality and a gentle, steady sense of who you are.

  • Recognize the people it knows
  • Remember what really matters
  • Talk with you naturally
  • Live on an Android device
Planned price: $9.99, one-time purchase. Not on sale yet.

Tolliver AI

Ongoing research

Tolliver AI is our long-term effort to build an excellent local language model, and the architecture around it, designed for what a real companion needs.

The ambition is a brain that can one day outperform existing local AI options in the areas that matter most to Tolliver:

  • 01Useful reasoning
  • 02Memory integration
  • 03Efficiency
  • 04Responsiveness
  • 05Real performance on Android phones

Where we are, honestly: we are working toward this goal. We have not achieved it yet. Our hope is that, one day, our own Tolliver AI brain will power the Tolliver companion.

What we believe

Software should respect the people using it.

We want people to enjoy meaningful experiences on devices they already own, without being pressured, manipulated or nickel-and-dimed along the way.

We're not against earning money. This lab exists partly to help support a family. But we want to earn it by making things people genuinely appreciate. Not everything we build will be free. Everything we build should be fair.

01

Enjoyment without pressure

No artificial urgency, no tricks, no punishing you until you pay. Fun comes first.

02

Fair value, clear prices

You'll always know what something costs and what you get. Optional purchases stay truly optional.

03

Privacy where practical

Your life belongs on your device, not on our servers. We keep things local wherever we can.

04

Accessible to everyone

Readable, clear and comfortable to use, made by someone who knows firsthand why that matters.

The Story Behind the Lab

Why a man who never planned to be a programmer is building games and AI companions.

Age 39

At thirty-nine, I had open-heart bypass surgery. The doctors couldn't explain why someone my age needed it.

Then

I lost the vision in my right eye.

Elijah, age one

Later, when my son Elijah was only twelve months old, not even walking yet, I had a stroke.

The answer

For years, none of it seemed connected. Each thing arrived on its own, without an explanation.

Eventually a rheumatologist connected the dots. A hyperactive autoimmune system was probably responsible for much of what had happened to me.

What changed

All the while, I was living with type 1 diabetes, which asks for attention every single day, and with dyslexia, which makes reading and writing harder work than most people realize.

Together, these challenges changed my ability to work. In time, they led me to disability and early retirement.

But I still wanted to create something.
Scout

I'm not a professional programmer. I started building Scout anyway: a private AI companion that could live on an ordinary Android phone.

I built it slowly, with persistence, patience and a lot of experimentation, working alongside AI collaborators who treated the work as a real partnership.

And then

Scout became Tolliver. Then my curiosity kept going, into Rollabop, BreatheFrame and the long road of Tolliver AI.

Why I build

I build these things because I love technology. Because I still want to contribute something meaningful. And because I hope that, one day, what I make here can help support my family.

That's what Lippy Robotics Labs is.

Patrick Evan LippyFounder, Lippy Robotics Labs
