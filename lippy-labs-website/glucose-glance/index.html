Features

Made to be easy to read

Everything is big, bright and clear, so you can check your glucose with one quick look.

  • Big number, clear arrowYour reading is very large, with a trend arrow beside it.
  • Colors and wordsGreen, yellow and red, plus the words "In range", "LOW" and "HIGH".
  • Read aloudTap the number to hear your reading spoken.
  • 3-hour dot chartSee where you have been, plus minutes since the last reading and the change, like +2 or -5.
  • Your own watch faceFree with the app: 10 backgrounds, 8 text colors and 4 spots you can change.
  • On any watch faceAdd your glucose to other watch faces that support complications, like many Samsung faces.

Before you buy

What you need

Your glucose service

  • Dexcom G6, G7 or ONE+ with Share turned on in your Dexcom app, and at least one follower invited. The follower can be a family member, or a second email address of your own.
  • Your Dexcom login must be an email or username. Dexcom Share does not work if your username is a phone number.
  • Or Nightscout: works with most other CGMs, such as Medtronic, Eversense, xDrip+, AndroidAPS and Loop. You need your site address, plus an access token if your site uses one.

Your watch

  • A Wear OS 3 or newer watch, such as a Samsung Galaxy Watch.
  • The Glucose Glance watch face needs Wear OS 4 or newer. On older watches you can still add your glucose to other watch faces.
  • Your watch needs an internet connection (through your phone or Wi-Fi) to get new readings, about every 5 minutes.
  • An Android phone to install the app on your watch.
  • For the one-time setup, your phone and watch must be on the same Wi-Fi network.
Getting started

How it works

Glucose Glance is free, and you get it here, not in the Google Play Store. Our simple phone app, the Glucose Glance Installer, sends Glucose Glance to your watch for you, step by step, with pictures. The installer is open source: view its source code.

  1. Get the installerOn your Android phone, tap Download free and open the file. If asked, allow installing from this source.
  2. Let the installer do the workIt connects to your watch and installs Glucose Glance and the free watch face, showing you each step with pictures.
  3. Sign in on your watchOpen Glucose Glance, choose Dexcom or Nightscout, and sign in. Then pick the Glucose Glance watch face.
Good to know before you start. Because Glucose Glance is not in the Play Store, the installer guides you to turn on Developer options and Wireless debugging on your watch. It is a one-time setup, with pictures. Your phone and watch need to be on the same Wi-Fi network, and you should keep Bluetooth on. When it is done, the installer shows you how to turn Wireless debugging off again.

Privacy and safety

Your data stays on your watch

Private by design. Your login stays on your watch, and your password or token is encrypted. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Lippy Robotics Labs has no servers and never receives your readings or your login. The app talks only to the glucose service you choose. Read the full privacy policy. It is also inside the app, under Settings, then About & Privacy.
Not a medical device. Glucose Glance shows a copy of readings from your glucose service, and those readings can be late, missing or wrong. Always keep your CGM's own app turned on for alarms, and do not make treatment decisions based on this app alone.
